Karan Mahotsav Celebration

Learn Bharat School celebrated Karan Mahotsav with great enthusiasm and joy. This vibrant festival promotes culture, knowledge, and learning. Students participated in songs, dances, plays, and competitions, guided by their teachers and cheered on by parents. The beautifully decorated school campus was alive with talent, unity, and happiness. The event inspires students to strive for excellence and reinforces values like discipline, respect, and cultural pride.
