Questions parents ask

Cannot find your answer? Send us a WhatsApp message.

Which classes do you offer?

We teach from Nursery to Class 9.

How do I apply?

Start with a WhatsApp message, a phone call or the enquiry form on this page. We will then invite you to visit the school and complete the formalities.

Can I visit the school before deciding?

Yes. We are happy to show you the classrooms and introduce you to our teachers. Book a visit on WhatsApp or by phone.

What does my child learn besides textbooks?

Activity-based learning, yoga, abacus and digital classrooms, along with sports, arts and community service.

How do I find out the fees?

Fees depend on the class. Message us on WhatsApp and we will share the current fee structure.
